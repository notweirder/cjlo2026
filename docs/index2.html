CJLO Funding Drive 2026!

From October 23rd to November 6th 2026, CJLO 1690AM is hosting our annual Funding Drive! Join us for two weeks of raising money, fun events and bringing the community together all in the name of our favourite station!!

We know you love CJLO as much as we do (that’s why you’re here!) This year we are raising money to help support and create job opportunities for students in media, develop and improve our new recording studio and so much more!

We are super excited to be collaborating with local businesses, organizations and venues to make this whole drive possible! We hope to see you out there at our events and don’t forget to tune in to 1690 AM and cjlo.com!
